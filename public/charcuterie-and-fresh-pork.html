Bert’s Pig Shop · the counter

Whole hog. Same building. No shortcuts.

Chef Bert Sheffield spent years in Asheville breaking down whole animals and fermenting what came off them. Bert’s Pig Shop is that craft pointed at one farm: Peculiar Pig, in Dorchester County.

The farm up the road

Peculiar Pig is a fifth-generation family farm on 12 acres of ancestral land near the Edisto River. Hogs graze and forage on wooded lots, moving paddock to paddock, finished on heritage grains. The farm raises whole hogs, primal cuts and sausages — and Bert’s Pig Shop exists to bring that meat home to West Ashley, fresh, with nothing wrapped in plastic from a warehouse three states away.

What the counter does

  • Fresh pork, by the cut the week’s breakdown, straight from the farm
  • House-made charcuterie rendered, cured and hung on site under our S.C. Department of Agriculture license
  • Boards the case, built to order for the table you are sitting at
  • Sandwiches what came off the hog, on bread, while you wait

Restaurant accounts around Charleston are next on the list — the shop was always meant to feed more than the dining room.
