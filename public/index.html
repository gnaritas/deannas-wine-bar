Deanna’s Wine Bar & Bert’s Pig Shop

A wine bar and a working pig shop in the same room — opening by the end of 2026 at 521 Wappoo Road, in the building that used to hold a 7‑Eleven.

The wine

Small producers, generous pours

Little-known bottles and revived classics from small European and domestic makers — at prices that turn a glass into a habit. The list is built by sommelier Andres Contreras, the man behind Rue de Jean’s 300‑bottle carte.

The pig shop

Charcuterie
made in this room

Bert’s Pig Shop works whole hogs from Peculiar Pig, a fifth-generation family farm in Dorchester County. Fresh pork, sausages and house-made charcuterie — rendered on site, under our state agriculture license.

The room

Cozy. Neighborhood. Dinner-party.

Two counters, one table. Order at the counter, get a seat, watch the boards come out of the same kitchen that breaks down the hogs. Sandwiches and boards on the menu from day one.
