A wine bar named for somebody’s mother

Deanna’s is named for Deanna Hickerson — the mother of Douglas Hickerson, one half of the pair behind this place. The other half is chef Bert Sheffield, and his family’s farm puts the pigs on the counter.

The two of them

Bert Sheffield was born in south Louisiana, cooked his way through Atlanta, then ran a kitchen in Asheville where he built a name for whole-animal butchery and fermentation. Douglas Hickerson is a Charleston real estate agent by day and a restaurateur by night — the pair kept circling each other’s plans until this corner lined up.

The room they chose

The building is the old 7‑Eleven on Wappoo Road, and they did not sandblast that fact away. The original floor stays. The painted green walkway stays — it is now the thin green line across the top of every page of this site. Before the deal was even signed, Bert put up a sign out front: hello, neighbors — something good is coming. If you have photos of the old store, they genuinely want to see them.

The bet

A wine bar that renders its own charcuterie, licensed to do it on the premises, stocked with wines from makers you can count on one hand, in a room built to feel like a dinner party instead of a tasting event. The owners figure it will be the only restaurant of its kind in South Carolina. We intend to be right.
