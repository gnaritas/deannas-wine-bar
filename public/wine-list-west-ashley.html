Deanna’s Wine Bar · the list

Wine chosen the hard way

Nearly three dozen tastings, a long dive through wine archives, and one rule: small makers at prices people around here will actually drink every week.

The list belongs to sommelier Andres Contreras, a West Ashley neighborhood wine program built on a résumé that starts with Rue de Jean — where he helped curate a 300‑bottle carte — and runs through Coast Bar & Grill and Costa. Charleston’s Zagat 30 Under 30 list says what the industry already knew; in 2017 he was the Charleston-based voice of the Wines of Chile campaign.

What to expect on it

  • Small European producers bottlings you will not find on the shelf at the big-box store two miles away
  • Small domestic makers the winemakers, not the wine brands
  • Revived and lesser-known bottles old favorites brought back, and new ones given their first Charleston pour
  • Affordable by design the whole point: a wine bar for Tuesday, not only for anniversaries

Order a glass or a bottle; it comes with sandwiches and house-made boards from the counter behind you. The full list posts here the week we open.
